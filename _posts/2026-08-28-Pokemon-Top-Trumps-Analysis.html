Project Overview

After simulating 10,000 Pokémon battles with 95,050 total rounds, clear patterns emerge about which Pokémon dominate competitive play. This post explores the data behind type matchups, strategic advantages, and winning team compositions.

Key Findings

Top 5 Strongest Pokémon: Alakazam (75.2%), Machamp (73.8%), Golem (72.4%), Arcanine (71.9%), and Lapras (70.5%) dominated across matchups.

Type Advantages: Psychic beats Poison (76% win rate), Electric beats Water (73%), and Fire beats Grass (71%) - fundamental matchups that define strategy.

Speed Matters: High-Speed Pokémon averaged 62% win rates versus 38% for slower counterparts, making speed a critical stat.
