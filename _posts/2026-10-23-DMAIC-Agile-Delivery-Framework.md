Continuous improvement work tends to fail in one of two ways: a great root-cause analysis that never ships, or fast delivery that solves the wrong problem. The framework I use links the two. **DMAIC decides _what_ to fix. Agile decides _how_ to deliver it.**

👉 **[Explore the interactive framework](https://flaakira.github.io/dmaic-agile-framework.html)**
👉 **[Get the templates on GitHub](https://github.com/flaakira/dmaic-agile-delivery-framework)**

![DMAIC + Agile framework](/img/posts/dmaic-agile-framework.png)

## 1. DMAIC finds the right problem

- **Define:** project charter and stakeholder mapping. What are we solving, for whom, and what does success look like?
- **Measure:** KPI baseline, measurement system analysis (MSA) and a data collection plan. Before trusting a number, check that it measures what you think it does.
- **Analyze:** root cause analysis, fishbone and 5 Whys, and critical factor identification.
- **Improve:** a prioritization chart (impact vs. effort), low-code solutions, requirements gathering and business requirements documents.
- **Control:** training, revamped processes, new KPIs and communication.

## 2. The bridge: solutions become features

In **Improve**, every prioritized solution becomes a **feature** in the Agile product backlog. Work is broken down as **Project → Feature → Story → Story points**:

| Points | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Done in less than a day |
| 2 | Done in 1 day |
| 3 | Done in 2 days |
| 5 | Done in a week |
| 8 | Done in 2 weeks |
| 13 | Too big: split it |

## 3. Agile delivers it in small pieces

Product backlog → sprint backlog → **sprints of 1–4 weeks** with a daily scrum → completed product. Each feature follows seven steps, each with clear owners:

1. **Requirements gathering:** Product Manager, Business Analyst
2. **BRD creation:** Business Analyst
3. **Product & sprint backlog:** CI Analyst, Product Manager
4. **Feature development:** BI Analyst
5. **UAT & demos:** CI Analyst, BI Analyst
6. **Training & comms:** Business Analyst, Product Manager
7. **Launch:** CI Analyst, Product Manager

**Control** closes the loop, and a new product launch ships about **every 2 months**.
