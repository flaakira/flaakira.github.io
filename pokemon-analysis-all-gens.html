🧪 Test Plan

Every game follows the live game's rules exactly: 6 v 6 knockout, a random stat each round, primary-type multipliers (1.2x / 0.8x), and the blocked-stat rule after a draw. Both sides pick stats at random, so the results measure the cards, not player skill.

🌍 Generation Comparison

Inside a single generation the match is fair: each side wins about half the time. The real difference shows when generations face each other. The newer generations (Gen 4, 7 and 9) carry higher stat totals and beat the original three generations about two times out of three.

📈 Stats Decide the Card

A card's stat total (Attack + Defense + Sp. Attack + Sp. Defense + Speed, HP is not used in the game) predicts its win rate almost perfectly: the correlation is 0.98. Type matters at the margins, but a big stat total matters most.
